Shipping Policy

Last Updated: December 2025

1. General Shipping Terms

Shipping costs vary by auction, item size, weight, insurance requirements, and destination. Buyers are responsible for all shipping, handling, and insurance fees unless otherwise stated.

2. Processing Time

Most orders ship within 7–21 business days after receipt of full payment and — for firearms — valid FFL information. Processing time may vary depending on auction volume and item type.

3. Lost or Damaged Shipments

Castleland Management, LLC is not liable for:

  • Items lost, delayed, or damaged in transit
  • Carrier errors or failures
  • Packages confiscated due to state or federal restrictions

Insurance is available upon request. Claims must be filed directly with the carrier.

4. Firearms Shipping

All modern firearms must be shipped to a valid Federal Firearms License (FFL) holder. Buyers must supply their receiving FFL's contact information within a reasonable timeframe.

5. Ammunition Shipping Restrictions

We do not ship ammunition to the following states or locations:

  • Alaska
  • Hawaii
  • California
  • New York City
  • Washington, D.C.
  • Illinois (certain areas)
  • Any location where prohibited by law

Buyers must be 21 years old to purchase handgun ammunition and 18 years old for rifle ammunition.

We reserve the right to require age verification before shipment.

Refunds & Cancellations Policy

Last Updated: December 2025

1. All Sales Are Final

Due to the nature of auctions, all bids and purchases are final and binding. No refunds, returns, or exchanges will be issued.

2. Order Cancellation

Castleland Management, LLC reserves the right to cancel any transaction that:

  • Violates federal, state, or local law
  • Cannot be completed due to missing FFL or required documentation
  • Appears fraudulent or suspicious

If Castleland Management, LLC cancels a transaction due to regulatory issues, any refund will exclude:

  • Buyer's premium
  • Credit card fees
  • Shipping or handling charges
  • Administrative costs

3. Chargebacks

Unauthorized chargebacks may result in suspension of bidding privileges and/or legal action.
