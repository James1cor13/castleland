Retired Air Force Pilot and WW2 Vet House Clean Out!

COMING SOON – MASSIVE ESTATE AUCTION SERIES!

We're beyond excited to announce the first installment of a multi-part estate auction from an incredible house cleanout in Chesterland, Ohio!

The former owner was in his 90s, a proud retired Air Force pilot, former airport owner, and flight instructor—and his home was absolutely packed with treasures from decades past. As we uncovered layer after layer, we discovered an unbelievable mix of vintage toys, old tools, military memorabilia, antiques, aviation items, and rare collectibles tucked away in every corner.

There's simply too much to fit into one sale, so we're splitting this estate into at least three separate auctions, each filled with hundreds of unique lots!

Whether you're a collector, reseller, or vintage enthusiast, this is one you won't want to miss.

Stay tuned and get ready to bid—these treasures are straight out of a time capsule!
